Prompt System Used in This Project

---

Pillar Blog Prompt

Act as an SEO expert and content strategist.

Create a long-form SEO blog for a Digital Marketing Agency in Bangalore targeting the keyword:
"Digital Marketing Agency in Bangalore"

Requirements:

* 1200–1500 words
* Use H1, H2, H3 structure
* Include introduction, services, pricing, benefits, how to choose, FAQs, and CTA
* Use simple English
* Include Bangalore naturally for local SEO
* Add internal linking suggestions
* Make it conversion-focused

---

Supporting Blog Prompt

Act as an SEO content writer.

Create a detailed SEO blog targeting the keyword:
"[INSERT KEYWORD]"

Requirements:

* 800–1000 words
* Use clear H1, H2, H3 headings
* Use simple and beginner-friendly English
* Answer search intent clearly
* Include Bangalore where relevant
* Add FAQ section
* Add call-to-action
* Include internal linking to pillar blog

---

Content Cluster Prompt

Act as an SEO strategist.

Create a content cluster for:
"Digital Marketing Agency in Bangalore"

Include:

* 1 pillar blog topic
* 4 supporting blog topics
* Keywords for each
* Search intent
* Internal linking strategy

Format in a table.
